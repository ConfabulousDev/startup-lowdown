# Capital

Answer: how much has the company raised, from whom, at what valuations, and what revenue does it claim? Runway and prospects are judged in synthesis, not here.

## Must-do checks

1. **The company's own funding statements**: announcements, job posts, and its YC profile ("we just raised"), then the lead investor's post, then business press.
2. **If acquired:** the deal filing or announcement in full (price, what was bought, what the acquirer said it plans for the business) and the acquirer's own filings.

Then, budget permitting: aggregators (Crunchbase, PitchBook, CB Insights, Dealroom) via snippets as leads to verify, and secondary marks (Forge, Caplight, EquityZen, Hiive) labeled as indicative.

## Rounds and valuation

One finding per round: date, round, amount, lead(s) separate from other participants, valuation if reported, and a source per round. Try the lead investor's post and other outlets before writing "lead unknown". Record any later mark below the last valuation (secondary price, down round, sale price).

## Revenue

Company and founder figures, including growth multiples ("4x in 2024"), are `claimed`; record every one with its date so synthesis can chain them. Third-party revenue estimates (GetLatka, Sacra, Owler) often belong to a same-name company.
